2) CHE DO TV (TvActivity)
- XOA phan "Nhat ky khoi dong" khoi man hinh TV.
  (Cfg.log() van chay nen de chan doan, chi khong hien nua.)
- XOA nut "Chan Cai dat TV" (xem muc 4).
- CHAY NEN KHI THU NHO - da kiem tra lai, ON:
  * Nut "Thu nho app" va phim Back chi goi moveTaskToBack(true),
    KHONG finish()/finishAndRemoveTask() (finish se bi he thong coi nhu
    vuot xoa da nhiem va dung luon dich vu).
  * Truoc khi thu nho: Cfg.ensureService() + KeepService.start()
    de dich vu foreground giu TvService song.
  * KeepService (START_STICKY, foreground) + WakeJob + BootReceiver giu app
    song khi TV tat/bat lai; KeepService tu tao lai vong lap Firebase neu
    treo > 60 giay; SCREEN_ON -> restartLoop (khong mo app).
  * Khi TV vua bat, TvActivity tu mo o che do thu nho (minimize) nhu cu.

4) BAO VE TV - DA XOA 3 PHAN, CAI DAT TV LUON MO
- Da xoa khoi ParentActivity: "Mo Cai dat TV 10 phut", "Chan Cai dat TV",
  "Tat chan Cai dat TV (mo tu do)".
- Da xoa khoi TvActivity: nut "Chan Cai dat TV: DANG BAT/TAT...".
- Da xoa khoi TvService: lenh "allowsettings", ham isProtected(),
  toan bo logic bam HOME khi mo Cai dat / PackageInstaller /

- Da xoa khoi MainActivity: extra ADB guard_settings / allow_min.
- Da xoa khoi status: truong "allow" va dong "Cai dat TV dang duoc MO".
- Ket qua: Cai dat TV luon mo, khong bi chan gi ca.

5) NHAT KY XEM TV HOM NAY - MOI (WatchLog.java)
- File moi: app/src/main/java/com/tvguard/app/WatchLog.java
- TV day su kien len Firebase node /watchlog.json:
  {"date":"2026-11-14","events":[{"t":"08:12","kind":"open"},...]}
  * "open": khi dich vu Tro nang ket noi (TvService.onServiceConnected),
    khi man hinh sang lai (KeepService SCREEN_ON).
  * "close": khi man hinh tat (KeepService SCREEN_OFF),
    khi sap tat nguon (TvService.powerOff()).
  * Chong ghi trung: cung loai su kien trong 60 giay thi bo qua.
    Qua ngay moi tu xoa danh sach cu, chi giu 60 su kien gan nhat.
- Dien thoai doc /watchlog.json moi 4 giay (cung vong poller status)
  va hien trong card NHAT KY XEM TV HOM NAY, dang:
  08:12 - Mo TV
  12:30 - Tat TV
  (neu chua co: "Hom nay chua co lich su Mo / Tat TV").
