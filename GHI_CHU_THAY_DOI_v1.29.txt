TV Guard - App 1 - v1.29 - GUI TIN NHAN + AM LUONG TV
=========================================================
1) GUI TIN NHAN LEN MAN HINH TV (che do dien thoai)
- Card moi "GUI TIN NHAN LEN MAN HINH TV", dat DUOI card Am luong TV
  va TREN card Gui anh len man hinh TV.
- O nhap nhieu dong, gioi han 300 ky tu (android:maxLength="300"),
  co bo dem "0/300" cap nhat khi go.
- Nut "Gui tin nhan" -> lenh showmsg (kem text) qua Firebase /cmd.json.
  Nut "An tin nhan" -> lenh hidemsg (phong khi khong co dieu khien).
- TV hien tin nhan toan man hinh: nen mo, chu trang to, kem dong
  "Bam Back hoac Home de tat tin nhan" o duoi.
- Thoat: chi can bam Back hoac Home tren dieu khien TV -> TvService
  chan 2 phim nay o onKeyEvent (dich vu Tro nang da co
  flagRequestFilterKeyEvents) va tat lop tin nhan, KHONG thoat app
  dang xem, KHONG ve Home. Cac phim khac (am luong, mui ten...)
  van di qua binh thuong khi tin nhan dang hien.
- Trang thai TV (status.json) them "msg": dien thoai hien dong
  "Dang hien thi tin nhan"; man hinh TV cung hien dong nay.
- Neu TV dang KHOA (lop khoa dang phu), tin nhan se nam sau lop khoa;
  bam Back/Home van tat duoc tin nhan.

2) AM LUONG TV (che do dien thoai)
- Card moi "AM LUONG TV" voi 2 nut "Tang am luong" / "Giam am luong",
  dat TREN khu vuc Dieu Khien Tuc Thi (cung la tren card Tin nhan).
- Lenh volup / voldn -> TV dung AudioManager.adjustStreamVolume(
  STREAM_MUSIC, ADJUST_RAISE/LOWER, FLAG_SHOW_UI) nen thanh am luong
  he thong cua TV cung hien len. Van chay duoc ca khi TV dang khoa.

3) TRANG THAI TV: DA XOA dong "Phim cuoi bi chan khi khoa: ..."
   cho gon khu vuc nay (TV van day "key" len Firebase, chi khong
   hien nua).

Thu tu card moi tren dien thoai: Trang thai TV -> Chup man hinh TV
-> Hen gio -> AM LUONG TV -> GUI TIN NHAN -> Gui anh -> Dieu Khien
Tuc Thi -> Icon app TV -> Nhat ky xem TV hom nay -> Cai dat.
