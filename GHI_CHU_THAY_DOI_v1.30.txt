TV Guard - App 1 - v1.30 - DOI VI TRI CARD DIEU KHIEN TUC THI
=========================================================
- Card "DIEU KHIEN TUC THI" (Khoa ngay / Mo khoa) da duoc doi len:
  nam TREN card "HEN GIO XEM TV" va DUOI card "CHUP MAN HINH TV".
- Cac card khac giu nguyen thu tu.

Thu tu card moi tren dien thoai: Trang thai TV -> Chup man hinh TV
-> DIEU KHIEN TUC THI -> Hen gio -> AM LUONG TV -> GUI TIN NHAN
-> Gui anh -> Icon app TV -> Nhat ky xem TV hom nay -> Cai dat.

Khong doi logic, khong doi chuc nang.
